About me

Turning ideas into practical software.

I am an Information Technology undergraduate at SLIIT, focusing on full-stack development, mobile applications, and UI/UX. I completed a frontend internship at Yilai Network Technology, where I built React interfaces in Vite, including booking UI, CRUD flows, and ticket management for the Smart Campus system.

I build web systems with React.js, Next.js, Node.js, Express.js, Java, and Spring Boot, and I use MySQL and MongoDB for data storage. SupportFlow AI is my current MVP: an agent workspace with Python, FastAPI, and retrieval-augmented answers. I design interfaces in Figma and test software with Playwright, TestNG, and Postman.

I also contributed to an AR-IoT research project on sensor placement simulation in smart greenhouses, and I am working toward a career as a software engineer with an emphasis on structured applications, reliable APIs, and usable interfaces.
